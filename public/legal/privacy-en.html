01

Who we are and what this covers

Hatef is the search and profile platform at hatef.ir. This policy explains how information is processed when you search, submit a website, create or visit a profile, or contact us. Questions and data requests can be sent to hatef.rostamkhani@gmail.com.

Ordinary web search does not require a Google connection. The Google Contacts and Gmail provisions below apply only if the optional invitation feature is made available and you separately authorize it. Publishing this policy does not enable those integrations.

02

Information we process

Search and service requests. Search terms, submitted URLs, request times, IP addresses, browser and device information, referring pages, and diagnostic or security logs may be processed to deliver results, operate the service and investigate abuse. We do not promise that searches or technical logs are never stored.

Profiles and communications. Information you provide can include your name, biography, skills, work history, photos, links, contact details, account identifiers and support messages. Public profile content can be viewed by visitors, indexed by search engines and copied by others. Review what you publish.

03

Profile analytics and browser storage

Profile view analytics record approximate location and general browser, operating system and device categories. The analytics record does not contain the raw IP address. Separate security, audit and compliance logs can contain identifying technical information, including IP addresses; separating analytics does not make all service use anonymous.

Features may use cookies, local storage or session storage for preferences, access credentials, editor state and unsaved drafts. Drafts or credentials may remain on the device after a tab closes. On a shared device, sign out where available and clear site data when finished. These legal pages do not load advertising or third-party analytics scripts.

04

Optional Google Contacts access

If the invitation feature is available, contact access is requested in context, separately from ordinary search. With your consent, the Google People API and contacts.readonly permission access contact names and email addresses so you can choose recipients. This permission does not allow editing or deleting contacts.

Contact data is used only for your requested invitation workflow. A full address book is not to be retained as a permanent contact database, published in profiles or the search index, sold, used for advertising, or used to invite people without your approval.

05

Optional email sending with Gmail

If you choose Gmail sending, a separate gmail.send permission allows sending from your Gmail account. You must see and approve the recipients and message before sending. This permission does not grant inbox-reading access. The recipients and Google receive the email and its usual sender information.

Connecting an account is not consent to recurring invitations. Scheduled or background sending requires a separate, explicit choice identifying recipients and messages and a way to stop pending sends. Google account limits and anti-spam rules apply. Hatef does not request your Google password.

06

Storage, retention and deletion

Profile data remains while the profile is maintained. Removing or unpublishing a profile hides it from normal public access; soft-deleted records, security logs, backups and copies held by other sites may remain. You can request deletion and ask which records must be retained.

The project documents a two-year retention target for profile analytics. Profile compliance records are assigned a 365-day expiry and are eligible for cleanup after expiry, unless held for an investigation. Actual erasure depends on cleanup jobs and the deployed configuration. Other operational logs and backups follow their operational or legal retention needs. Contact us for information about a particular record.

When a Google integration is enabled, access tokens, any refresh token and the minimum approved invitation data must be protected and retained only as needed for the authorized connection and task. Disconnecting must stop future access and invalidate or remove stored tokens. Contact-list cache and pending invitation data must be removed when no longer needed. Revoking access does not recall delivered email or automatically erase previously retained records.

07

Sharing and limited use

We do not sell personal information. Hosting, storage, network, email and security providers may process the information necessary to operate the requested service. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or needed to investigate abuse and protect users. Public profile information is public by your choice.

Hatef’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is not used for advertising, data brokerage, credit decisions or training generalized AI models. Transfers are limited to the user-facing feature with consent, security, applicable legal requirements, or an ownership transfer with prior explicit consent where required by Google policy.

Human access to Google data is limited to your affirmative agreement to view specific data, necessary security investigation, legal obligations, or permitted aggregated internal operations. Staff and service providers must follow the same restrictions.

08

Your choices and requests

You decide whether to publish a profile or connect Google. You can revoke authorization in Google Account connections. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, export, deletion or restriction of your personal information. Send your request to hatef.rostamkhani@gmail.com. We may verify control of the account before acting.

An invitation recipient can contact the same address to report an unwanted invitation or request that further invitations stop. Where a feature provides an unsubscribe or cancellation control, use it directly.

09

Security and external services

We use access controls and safeguards appropriate to the information being processed. The project includes encryption for designated sensitive fields and compliance records. This is not a guarantee that every log or backup is encrypted, or that the service is immune to a security incident.

Following a search result or an external link takes you to another service with its own privacy practices. We do not control third-party copies, search caches, or emails already delivered to recipients.

10

Updates and contact

Material changes are published here with an updated revision date. A new purpose for Google data outside your original authorization requires notice and appropriate new consent before that use begins. For privacy questions, data requests or concerns about this policy, contact hatef.rostamkhani@gmail.com.
