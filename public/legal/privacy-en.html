Introduction and scope

Hatef is a web search service and related platform available at hatef.ir. This policy explains how data is handled in search, profiles and optional Google integrations. Connecting a Google Account is not required for ordinary web search.

Information we process

Depending on the feature used, information may include search queries, account or profile details you provide, preferences, browser and device information, IP addresses, request timestamps and data needed for security, troubleshooting and aggregated analytics. Information you publish in a public profile may be visible to other visitors and indexed by search engines.

Google Contacts permission

Only when you choose the contact invitation feature and separately consent, Hatef uses the Google People API with the read-only contacts.readonly scope to access contact names and email addresses so that you can review and choose recipients. This scope does not permit Hatef to edit or delete your contacts.

Contact information must only be used for the feature you requested, not for sale, targeted advertising, unrelated contact databases or invitations without your approval.

Sending invitations with Gmail

If you choose to send invitations and separately grant gmail.send, Hatef can send email from your Gmail account after showing you the message contents and recipients for approval. This scope does not allow Hatef to read your Gmail inbox. Connecting Google alone is not permission for unlimited or automated sending. Any future scheduling feature will require a separate, explicit choice.

Purposes, retention and sharing

Information is processed to deliver requested features, provide support, protect service security, investigate errors and comply with applicable legal obligations. The invitation feature must avoid permanent retention of the complete contact list. OAuth access must no longer be usable after a connection is removed. Operational data is retained only as needed for the stated purposes or applicable legal obligations.

Infrastructure service providers may process data only to the extent necessary to deliver services. Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold to data brokers or used for personalized advertising. Hatef's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Security and your choices

Access controls and secure connections are intended to protect information. No online system can guarantee absolute security. You can revoke Hatef's Google access in your Google Account connections. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information associated with your Hatef account. Revoking Google access does not by itself necessarily delete information previously retained on a lawful basis.

Changes and contact

If an important new use of Google data exceeds the scope of your original consent, we will provide notice and obtain appropriate consent before that new use. For privacy questions and data requests, email hatef.rostamkhani@gmail.com.
